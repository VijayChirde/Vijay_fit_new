Your 180-day roadmap

Five balanced resistance sessions per week, walking and planned recovery. No forced advanced training.

Weekly training structure

Progression rules

  1. Start with a comfortable load selected with a qualified trainer.
  2. For the first two weeks, perform two working sets per exercise to learn technique.
  3. When all sets reach the top of the rep range with good form and around two reps in reserve, the app suggests the smallest available increase for the next session.
  4. If reps fall below range, keep the load or reduce it. Never chase the suggestion.
  5. Pain, unusual breathlessness, dizziness or headache means stop; do not progress that exercise.
  6. Training phases advance by readiness and review, not calendar alone.
